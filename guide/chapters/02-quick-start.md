# Quick Start

::: lead
Get your Hub connected and your first dashboard working. Leave optional features for later.
:::

## Before you begin

Have the correct cable or adapter for your RV, one supported power source, and a phone or tablet. If you are unsure about the wiring, see [Set Up Your Hub](#set-up-your-hub) and the [installation FAQ](https://launchcontrol.tech/pages/support-faq) before connecting anything.

::: warning "Connect safely"
Disconnect power before wiring or changing connections. Power the Hub from either 12–24 VDC or USB-C, never both. Use the correct RV-C connector and adapter for your RV; never guess at wire functions.
:::

## Get your first dashboard working

::: warning "Before loading a floor plan or testing equipment"
Back up a Hub you have already customized before loading a floor plan: loading replaces the current configuration. Keep people and objects clear of moving equipment. Test controls only when you can see the equipment and confirm it is safe to operate.
:::

1. **Connect the Hub.** Connect the RV-C cable, then connect the supported power source. If your installation uses only separate equipment such as Victron or Bluetooth, follow the relevant integration instructions instead of connecting RV-C.
2. **Connect your phone.** Join the Wi-Fi network named LaunchControl-Hub-XXXX. The setup wizard should open. If it does not, visit [192.168.4.1](http://192.168.4.1) in your browser.
3. **Follow the setup wizard.** Confirm your units and time zone. Connect to your RV’s Wi-Fi router if you have one, or choose **Set Up Without Wi-Fi**. Remote access can wait. Once the dashboard opens, its seven-step setup guide helps you finish.
4. **Find your equipment.** Choose **Load a Floorplan** if a matching configuration is available, or **Find equipment** to scan. To identify a light or pump, operate its original RV switch and watch the list. If scanning, name the devices, choose what each switched circuit does, then press **Add Equipment & Create Dashboard**. Add only equipment you recognize. If your installation has no built-in RV-C equipment, use the Additional equipment step for your integrations instead. See [Add or Change Equipment](#add-or-change-equipment) for help.
5. **Finish the setup guide.** Under Additional equipment, select only the equipment you actually use, or **None**. Follow the relevant setup prompts. Displays and Remote are optional. Choose **Open dashboard** when the guide says **LaunchControl is ready**.
6. **Check and protect your Hub.** Check that readings make sense and that each control operates the intended equipment safely. Set a Dashboard PIN and hotspot password under Settings → Network before pairing displays or sharing access. See [Security](#security).

::: note "Your phone may report no internet"
The Hub’s own Wi-Fi is a local network, not an internet service. Stay connected to it when prompted. If the setup page stops loading, check that your phone has not switched to another network.
:::



## You’re ready

If your expected readings and controls work and the Hub is protected, basic setup is finished. You can stop here. Visit [Controls](#controls) when you want to learn the everyday gestures, or [The Dashboard](#the-dashboard) when you want to customize the layout.

If a step does not work, [Set Up Your Hub](#set-up-your-hub) has the detailed instructions and [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting) helps you find the cause.
